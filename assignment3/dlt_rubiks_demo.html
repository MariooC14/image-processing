DLT Calibration & Decomposition Algorithm

⚠ Implementation Note: This implementation does NOT use normalization (MATLAB style DLT). Raw coordinates are used for both 2D and 3D points.

Phase 1: Data Collection

  1. Collect Correspondences: Select at least 6 non-coplanar 3D points and their matching 2D image points.

Phase 2: Linear System Setup

  1. 2. Build DLT Matrix: Construct the linear system A from the correspondences for P (camera matrix).
  2. 3. Solve for P: Use SVD to solve $A \mathbf{p} = 0$ for $\mathbf{p}$ (vectorized $P$), take the last column of $V$.
  3. 4. Reshape P: Reshape $\mathbf{p}$ to $P$ (3×4 matrix).

Phase 3: Matrix Decomposition

  1. 5. Decompose P: Partition $P = [M | p_4]$ where $M$ is 3×3, $p_4$ is translation.
  2. 6. RQ/QR Decomposition: Decompose $M$ to get $K$ (intrinsic) and $R$ (rotation). Normalize $K$ so $K[2,2]=1$.
  3. 7. Fix Reflection: If $\det(R) < 0$, flip signs of $K[2,:]$ and $R[:,2]$ to ensure proper rotation.

Phase 4: Extrinsic Parameters & Validation

  1. 8. Compute t: $t = K^{-1} p_4$; scale $t$ by dividing by $K[2,2]$ if $K$ was normalized.
  2. 9. Camera Center C: $C = -R^T t$ (the true camera center in world coordinates).
  3. 10. Orientation Check: If camera faces away from scene, flip $R$ and $t$ so $-R[2,:]$ points toward the scene center.
📚 References: Hartley & Zisserman, Multiple View Geometry; MATLAB Camera Calibration Toolbox; OpenCV camera calibration docs.
